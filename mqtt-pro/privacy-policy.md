# MQTT Pro: Privacy Policy

**Effective 26 October 2026** · Publisher: HSI Lighting · Applies to the MQTT Pro app for iPhone and iPad

## Short version

MQTT Pro does not collect, store or share your personal data with us or anyone else. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What the app does with your data

MQTT Pro is a client for MQTT message brokers. Everything below stays on your device, except the connections you set up yourself.

- **Broker connections.** The app connects only to the MQTT brokers you add. The messages you send and receive travel between your device and that broker. We do not operate the broker and cannot see this traffic. Your broker's operator has its own privacy practices.
- **Connection details.** Host names, ports and settings are stored on your device. Passwords you choose to save are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
- **Message history and recordings.** Received and sent messages may be kept on your device for the number of days set in Settings, so you can review them. They never leave the device unless you export or share them yourself (for example a session export or bug-report bundle).
- **Labels and rules.** Names you give to senders and rules you write are stored on your device only.
- **Notifications.** Any alerts are generated on your device. The app does not use a push notification server.
- **Local network.** iOS may ask permission so the app can reach brokers on your local network. The app uses this only to connect to brokers you configured.
- **Version check.** When you open About & updates, the app downloads a small public file that states the latest version number, from `raw.githubusercontent.com` (GitHub). Like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address. The app sends no other information with it.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect personal data, device identifiers or usage statistics.
- We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
- We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

## Children

The app is a technical tool, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone.
